## Resting energy expenditure
The Mifflin-St Jeor equation estimates resting energy expenditure (REE) from weight, height, age and sex: for men REE = 10 x weight (kg) + 6.25 x height (cm) - 5 x age (years) + 5; for women the constant is -161 instead of +5. In evidence reviews by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics it was the most accurate of the common predictive equations for healthy adults, including adults with obesity, predicting REE within about 10% of measured values for most people.

## From resting to total energy
Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) is estimated by multiplying REE by a physical activity factor: about 1.2 for sedentary people, 1.375 for light activity (exercise 1-3 days a week), 1.55 for moderate activity (3-5 days), 1.725 for very active people (6-7 days) and 1.9 for extremely active people or physical jobs. These factors are approximations; real needs vary by several hundred kilocalories, so intake should be adjusted against weight trends over 2-4 weeks.

## Limits
Predictive equations are not validated for children and adolescents, pregnancy, or people with major illness, where measured or clinician-set targets are preferred.
