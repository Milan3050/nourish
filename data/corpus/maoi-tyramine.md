## Interaction
Monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) such as phenelzine, tranylcypromine and isocarboxazid stop the body from breaking down tyramine. Large amounts of tyramine can then cause a sudden, dangerous rise in blood pressure (hypertensive crisis), with severe headache, palpitations and nausea.

## Foods high in tyramine
Aged cheeses (such as cheddar, parmesan and blue cheese), cured or fermented meats (salami, pepperoni), fermented soy products (soy sauce, miso), sauerkraut and kimchi, tap or unpasteurised beer, broad (fava) bean pods, yeast extracts and foods that are spoiled or improperly stored. Fresh foods are generally low in tyramine.

## Advice
People on MAOIs should follow the low-tyramine diet given by their prescriber, including for about two weeks after stopping the medicine.
