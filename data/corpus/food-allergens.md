## Major allergens
In the US, nine major food allergens account for most serious reactions: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame (added in 2023). India's FSSAI labelling rules also require declaration of common allergens such as cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, milk, eggs, fish, peanuts, tree nuts and soy.

## Hidden sources
Allergens often appear under other names: whey, casein and ghee (milk); albumin (egg); semolina, suji, maida and atta (wheat); tahini and til (sesame); groundnut oil (peanut). Cross-contact during manufacturing is common, so "may contain" warnings matter for severe allergies.

## Safety
There is no cure for food allergy; strict avoidance is the main treatment. People with a history of anaphylaxis should carry prescribed epinephrine. Lactose intolerance is a digestive condition, not an allergy, and many people with it tolerate small amounts of lactose or fermented dairy such as curd.
