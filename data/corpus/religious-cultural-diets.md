## Halal
Halal diets exclude pork and pork products, alcohol, and meat not slaughtered according to Islamic law. Gelatin and some additives may come from pork; halal-certified products avoid this.

## Kosher
Kosher diets exclude pork and shellfish, require meat from permitted animals slaughtered in a prescribed way, and do not combine meat and dairy in the same meal.

## Jain
Jain diets are vegetarian and also exclude root and bulb vegetables (potato, onion, garlic, carrot, beetroot, radish, ginger), because harvesting them destroys the whole plant; many also avoid mushrooms, honey and eating after sunset. Many Hindus avoid beef, and some avoid all meat, eggs, onion and garlic on fasting days.

## Personalisation
Plans should treat religious and cultural rules as hard constraints and suggest familiar dishes from the person's cuisine.
