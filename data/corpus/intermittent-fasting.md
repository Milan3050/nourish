## Evidence
Randomised trials comparing time-restricted eating with ordinary calorie restriction have generally found similar weight loss when total calories are matched. For example, a 2022 trial in the New England Journal of Medicine found that adding an 8-hour eating window to calorie restriction did not produce greater weight loss over 12 months.

## Who should avoid it
Fasting regimens are not appropriate for people with a history of eating disorders, pregnant or breastfeeding women, children and adolescents, or people with diabetes who use insulin or sulfonylureas, unless supervised.

## Practical view
Meal timing is a matter of preference; regular meals that fit a person's schedule and total energy needs are what matter most.
