## Calcium
The RDA for calcium is 1,000 mg per day for adults aged 19-50 and for men up to 70, and 1,200 mg for women over 50 and everyone over 70. The upper limit is 2,500 mg per day for adults aged 19-50 and 2,000 mg after 50. Dairy products are the richest common sources; others include fortified plant milks, calcium-set tofu, ragi (finger millet), sesame seeds, leafy greens such as amaranth, and small fish eaten with bones.

## Vitamin D
The RDA for vitamin D is 15 micrograms (600 IU) per day for adults up to 70 and 20 micrograms (800 IU) after 70. Few foods contain much vitamin D: fatty fish, egg yolks and fortified milk are the main sources, and sun exposure contributes. Deficiency is widespread in India despite abundant sunlight.
