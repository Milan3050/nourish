## Caffeine
For healthy adults, the FDA cites 400 mg per day (about four or five cups of brewed coffee) as an amount not generally associated with negative effects. People who are pregnant should limit caffeine to under 200 mg per day. A cup of Indian filter coffee or chai contains roughly 40-100 mg depending on strength. Caffeine can interact with some medicines and disturb sleep.

## Water
The National Academies set adequate total water intake (from all drinks and food) at about 3.7 litres per day for men and 2.7 litres for women, with needs rising in heat and with exercise. Plain water is the best choice; sugar-sweetened drinks add calories without fullness.
