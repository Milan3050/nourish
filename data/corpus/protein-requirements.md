## Baseline requirement
The Recommended Dietary Allowance for protein for healthy adults is 0.8 g per kg of body weight per day. This prevents deficiency in nearly all healthy adults but is not necessarily optimal for active people or during weight loss.

## Exercise and muscle gain
The International Society of Sports Nutrition concludes that 1.4 to 2.0 g of protein per kg per day is sufficient for most exercising people to build and maintain muscle, spread across meals of about 0.25 g/kg (20-40 g) every 3-4 hours. During an energy deficit, intakes towards the higher end (around 1.6 g/kg or more) help preserve lean mass.

## Cautions
In people with obesity, protein targets are often based on an adjusted or reference body weight rather than total weight. People with chronic kidney disease may need protein restriction set by their nephrologist or renal dietitian, so high-protein plans are not appropriate without clinical advice.
