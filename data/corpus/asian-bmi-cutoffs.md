## Lower thresholds
Asian populations, including South Asians, have a higher risk of type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease at lower BMI than European populations. A WHO expert consultation identified additional public-health action points at a BMI of 23 and 27.5 kg/m2.

## Indian consensus
Indian consensus guidelines define overweight as a BMI of 23.0-24.9 and obesity as 25 or above, and recommend waist circumference cut-offs of 90 cm for men and 80 cm for women for abdominal obesity.

## Use
These cut-offs help interpret risk; weight goals should still be set with the person's health, body composition and preferences in mind.
