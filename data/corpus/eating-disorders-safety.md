## What they are
Eating disorders, including anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa and binge-eating disorder, are serious and sometimes fatal illnesses involving severe disturbances in eating behaviour and related thoughts and emotions. They can affect people of any age, gender, body size or background.

## Warning signs
Signs include extreme restriction of food, intense fear of gaining weight, very low body weight, compensatory behaviours such as vomiting, laxative misuse or excessive exercise, and recurrent episodes of eating large amounts with a sense of loss of control.

## Getting help
Early treatment improves outcomes. Calorie-restricted plans and weight-loss targets are not appropriate for someone showing these signs; the right step is to talk to a doctor or a mental health professional who specialises in eating disorders.
