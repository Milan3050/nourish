## Intake
WHO recommends less than 5 g of salt (under 2 g of sodium) per day for adults. Studies suggest average intake in India is around 8-11 g per day, well above this.

## Hidden sources
Beyond salt added in cooking, major sources include pickles (achar), papad, namkeen and chips, instant noodles, sauces and ketchup, processed cheese, bakery products, and restaurant and street food. One tablespoon of pickle can contain a large fraction of the daily limit.

## Reducing salt
Cook with less salt and add it at the end, use lemon, herbs and spices for flavour, limit pickles and papad to occasional use, and read labels for sodium. Rock salt and pink salt contain about the same sodium as table salt.
