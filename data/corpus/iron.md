## Requirements
The US RDA for iron is 8 mg per day for adult men and post-menopausal women and 18 mg per day for women aged 19-50; pregnancy raises it to 27 mg. Vegetarians may need up to 1.8 times more because non-heme iron from plants is less well absorbed. Iron-deficiency anaemia is common in India, particularly among women and adolescent girls.

## Absorption
Heme iron from meat, poultry and fish is absorbed better than non-heme iron from plants and fortified foods. Vitamin C (citrus, amla, guava, tomatoes) eaten in the same meal increases non-heme iron absorption, while tea, coffee, calcium and phytates in the same meal reduce it. Taking tea or coffee between meals rather than with them helps.

## Upper limit
The tolerable upper intake level for adults is 45 mg per day from all sources. High-dose supplements can cause gastrointestinal upset and should only be taken when advised.
