## Vegetarian sources
Per 100 g raw, pulses such as moong dal, masoor dal, chana and rajma provide roughly 20-25 g of protein, soybean around 35-40 g, paneer around 18-19 g, and groundnuts about 23-25 g. Curd and milk provide 3-4 g per 100 g but are eaten in larger portions. Combining cereals with pulses (dal-rice, rajma-chawal, idli-sambar) improves protein quality.

## Non-vegetarian sources
Eggs provide about 13 g of protein per 100 g (6-7 g per egg). Chicken breast and lean fish provide roughly 20-23 g per 100 g raw. Fish such as rohu, pomfret and sardine are also good sources of other nutrients.

## Practical tips
Spreading protein across meals, adding a pulse or dairy item to each meal, and including sprouts, chana or curd as snacks help vegetarians reach higher protein targets without supplements.
