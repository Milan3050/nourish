## Quantity
Visible fat (oils, ghee, butter) adds about 9 kcal per gram and can push intake over energy needs quickly. Indian guidelines advise limiting visible oil and fat to roughly 20-30 g per day for most adults, depending on energy needs, and keeping total fat under about 30% of energy.

## Quality
Use a variety of vegetable oils to balance fatty acids, for example combining or rotating groundnut, mustard, rice bran, sesame and soybean oil. Ghee, butter, coconut oil and vanaspati are high in saturated fat, and vanaspati may contain trans fats; use them sparingly. Replacing saturated fat with unsaturated fats lowers LDL cholesterol.

## Cooking practice
Avoid reheating oil repeatedly for deep frying, as this produces harmful compounds, and prefer steaming, boiling, roasting and shallow cooking.
