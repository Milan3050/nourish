## Overall
Appropriately planned vegetarian, including vegan, diets are healthful, nutritionally adequate and suitable for all stages of life and for athletes, according to the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics.

## Nutrients to plan for
Vitamin B12 is found naturally only in animal foods, so vegans need fortified foods or a B12 supplement; lacto-vegetarians can get some from milk and curd but deficiency is still common in India. Other nutrients to watch are iron, zinc, calcium (for vegans), iodine, vitamin D and omega-3 fats.

## Protein
Plant proteins from a variety of sources across the day supply all essential amino acids. Combining cereals with pulses, such as rice with dal or roti with rajma, improves protein quality because cereals are low in lysine and pulses are low in methionine. Soy, paneer, curd, tofu, tempeh, lentils, chickpeas and peanuts are concentrated protein sources.
