## Interaction
Warfarin (Coumadin) works by antagonising vitamin K. Sudden increases in vitamin K intake can reduce warfarin's anticoagulant effect, and sudden decreases can increase bleeding risk. People taking warfarin should keep their vitamin K intake consistent from day to day rather than avoid vitamin K entirely, and should tell their clinician about large dietary changes.

## Food sources
Green leafy vegetables such as spinach, kale, collard greens, fenugreek and amaranth leaves are the richest sources of vitamin K1 (phylloquinone). Some vegetable oils (soybean, canola) and broccoli also contribute. The Adequate Intake is 120 micrograms per day for men and 90 micrograms per day for women.

## Other anticoagulants
Newer direct oral anticoagulants (such as apixaban and rivaroxaban) do not interact with vitamin K in the same way.
