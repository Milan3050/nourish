## Energy
Calorie needs do not increase in the first trimester. Roughly 340 extra kcal per day are needed in the second trimester and about 450 in the third. Weight-loss diets are not recommended during pregnancy; weight-gain targets depend on pre-pregnancy BMI and should be set with the obstetric care team.

## Key nutrients
Women who could become pregnant should take 400 micrograms of folic acid daily to reduce the risk of neural tube defects, continuing in early pregnancy. Iron needs rise to 27 mg per day, and iodine, choline, calcium, vitamin D and DHA are also important. A prenatal supplement is usually recommended.

## Food safety
Avoid alcohol, raw or undercooked meat, fish and eggs, unpasteurised milk and soft cheeses, and high-mercury fish. Limit caffeine to less than 200 mg per day. Breastfeeding raises energy needs by roughly 330-400 kcal per day.
