## Strict avoidance
People with celiac disease must avoid gluten, a protein in wheat, barley and rye, completely and for life. Even small amounts damage the small intestine. Oats are often contaminated with wheat; only oats labelled gluten-free are generally safe, and some people react even to those.

## Safe staples
Naturally gluten-free foods include rice, millets (jowar, bajra, ragi), maize, quinoa, buckwheat, amaranth, potatoes, pulses, dairy, eggs, meat, fish, fruits, vegetables, nuts and seeds. Indian staples made from wheat (roti, chapati, paratha, suji, maida, dalia) must be replaced, for example with rice, millet rotis or besan (chickpea flour).

## Nutrients
Gluten-free diets can be low in fiber, B vitamins and iron, so whole-grain gluten-free choices such as millets and pulses help.
