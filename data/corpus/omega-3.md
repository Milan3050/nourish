## Sources
The long-chain omega-3 fats EPA and DHA come mainly from fatty fish such as salmon, mackerel, sardines and Indian oil sardine (mathi). Plant foods such as flaxseed, chia, walnuts, mustard oil and soybean oil provide ALA, which the body converts to EPA and DHA only in small amounts.

## Recommendations
The American Heart Association recommends eating fish, particularly fatty fish, at least twice a week. The Adequate Intake for ALA is 1.6 g per day for men and 1.1 g for women. Vegetarians can include ground flaxseed, chia or walnuts daily; algae-based DHA supplements are an option for vegans.

## Mercury
Pregnant women and young children should choose low-mercury fish and avoid high-mercury fish such as shark, swordfish, king mackerel and bigeye tuna.
