## Healthy dietary pattern
Follow a healthy dietary pattern at every life stage, built from nutrient-dense foods: vegetables of all types, fruits (especially whole fruit), grains (at least half whole grains), dairy or fortified soy alternatives, protein foods (lean meats, poultry, eggs, seafood, beans, peas, lentils, nuts, seeds and soy products) and oils.

## Limits
Limit added sugars to less than 10% of calories per day from age 2, and avoid added sugars for children under 2. Limit saturated fat to less than 10% of calories per day from age 2. Limit sodium to less than 2,300 mg per day for adults and teens. Adults who drink alcohol should limit intake to 2 drinks or fewer per day for men and 1 drink or fewer for women, and drinking less is better for health than drinking more.

## Fiber
Dietary fiber is a nutrient of public health concern because most Americans do not eat enough. The guidelines base fiber goals on 14 g per 1,000 kcal, roughly 25 g per day for women and 31-34 g for men aged 19-50.
