## Weekly amount for adults
Adults aged 18-64 should do at least 150-300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity, or 75-150 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity, or an equivalent combination, across the week. Doing more than 300 minutes of moderate activity brings additional health benefits.

## Muscle strengthening
Adults should also do muscle-strengthening activities at moderate or greater intensity that involve all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week.

## Sitting less
Adults should limit sedentary time and replace it with physical activity of any intensity, including light intensity. Some activity is better than none, and people who are not active should start with small amounts and gradually increase frequency, intensity and duration.

## Special groups
Pregnant and postpartum women without contraindications should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity a week, under the guidance of their health-care provider. People with chronic conditions should be as active as their abilities allow and consult a professional about the type and amount suitable for them.
