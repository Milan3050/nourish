## Mechanism
Grapefruit and grapefruit juice can block the intestinal enzyme CYP3A4 that breaks down many drugs, so more of the drug enters the blood. Seville oranges and pomelos have a similar effect; ordinary sweet oranges do not.

## Affected drugs
Interactions are well documented for some statins (simvastatin, lovastatin and, to a lesser degree, atorvastatin), some blood-pressure drugs (such as felodipine and nifedipine), some anti-arrhythmics, some immunosuppressants (cyclosporine, tacrolimus) and some anti-anxiety drugs (buspirone). Rosuvastatin and pravastatin are less affected. Higher drug levels raise the risk of side effects such as muscle damage with statins.

## Advice
People taking an affected drug should ask their pharmacist or doctor whether they can have grapefruit, and should read the medication guide.
