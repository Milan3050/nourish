## Balanced diet
The guidelines estimate that a large share of India's disease burden is linked to unhealthy diets. They recommend a diverse diet drawing from multiple food groups each day: cereals and millets, pulses and legumes, vegetables including green leafy vegetables, fruits, milk and curd, nuts and oilseeds, with eggs, fish and meat for non-vegetarians.

## Limits
Over-reliance on cereals is discouraged; cereals and millets should supply less than about half of daily energy, with more coming from pulses, vegetables, fruits and dairy. Restrict salt intake, limit oils and fats, minimise sugar, and avoid ultra-processed foods high in fat, sugar and salt (HFSS). Reading food labels is encouraged.

## Plate model
The "My Plate for the Day" model fills a large share of the plate with vegetables and fruits, pairs cereals or millets with pulses, and adds a portion of milk or curd and a small amount of nuts and seeds.
