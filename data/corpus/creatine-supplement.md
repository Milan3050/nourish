## Evidence
Creatine monohydrate is one of the most studied sports supplements. It increases muscle creatine stores and can improve performance in repeated high-intensity exercise and gains in lean mass during resistance training. A common protocol is 3-5 g per day; a loading phase of about 20 g per day for 5-7 days is optional.

## Safety
In healthy people, short- and long-term use at recommended doses has not been shown to cause kidney damage or other serious adverse effects in controlled studies. Creatine can raise serum creatinine, which can be mistaken for reduced kidney function on blood tests. Small water-weight gains are common.

## Who should be cautious
People with existing kidney disease, or those taking drugs that affect the kidneys, should not use creatine without approval from their doctor. Evidence in adolescents, pregnancy and breastfeeding is limited.
