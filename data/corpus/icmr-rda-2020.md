## Protein
The 2020 ICMR-NIN report sets the protein RDA for healthy Indian adults at 0.83 g per kg of body weight per day, with an Estimated Average Requirement of 0.66 g/kg. It uses a reference body weight of 65 kg for men and 55 kg for women, giving about 54 g and 46 g per day respectively. Protein quality improves when cereals are combined with pulses in about a 3:1 ratio.

## Energy
Energy requirements are given by sex, age and three activity levels (sedentary, moderate, heavy), based on BMR equations and physical activity levels. Pregnancy adds about 350 kcal per day in the second and third trimesters, and lactation about 550-600 kcal in the first six months.

## Use
Recommended allowances are intakes that meet the needs of nearly all healthy people; individual targets may differ with body size, activity and health conditions.
