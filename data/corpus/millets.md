## Nutrition
Millets such as ragi (finger millet), jowar (sorghum), bajra (pearl millet), foxtail, kodo and little millet are rich in dietary fiber, minerals and B vitamins. Ragi is notably high in calcium (about 340-360 mg per 100 g), and bajra is a good source of iron.

## Health
Whole millets generally have a lower glycaemic response than polished rice or refined wheat flour and are naturally gluten-free, making them useful for people managing diabetes or coeliac disease. Processing (fine milling, instant mixes) reduces these benefits.

## Use
Millets can replace part of the rice or wheat in rotis, dosas, upma, khichdi and porridges. Introduce gradually with enough water to avoid digestive discomfort from the higher fiber.
