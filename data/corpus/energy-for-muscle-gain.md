## Energy surplus
Gaining muscle is easier with a modest energy surplus above maintenance, together with progressive resistance training. A small surplus limits fat gain compared with a large one; weight gain of roughly 0.25-0.5 percent of body weight per week is a common target for lean gains.

## Meals and timing
Spreading protein over 3-5 meals and including a protein source after training supports muscle protein synthesis. Energy-dense whole foods such as whole grains, milk and curd, nuts and seeds, legumes and healthy oils help reach a higher energy intake without very large meal volumes.

## Healthy weight range
Body mass index is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. For South Asian adults, Indian consensus guidance uses lower cut-offs (overweight from a BMI of 23), so a healthy weight range is calculated from height using BMI 18.5-22.9 rather than 18.5-24.9.
