## What it is
Lactose intolerance is a digestive condition in which the body produces too little lactase to digest lactose, the sugar in milk. It causes bloating, gas, abdominal pain and diarrhoea after dairy. It is different from milk allergy, which is an immune reaction to milk proteins.

## Managing it
Many people with lactose intolerance can drink small amounts of milk, especially with meals, and tolerate curd, yoghurt and hard cheeses, which contain less lactose. Lactose-free milk and lactase enzyme tablets are options.

## Nutrients
People who avoid dairy should get calcium and vitamin D from other sources such as fortified plant milks, calcium-set tofu, ragi, leafy greens and small fish with bones.
