## Frequency and volume
Training each major muscle group about twice a week works well for most people. For muscle growth, roughly 10-20 hard sets per muscle group per week is a common evidence-based range for trained people; beginners make good progress with fewer sets, about 6-12, and should build up gradually.

## Reps and rest
Loads that allow about 6-15 repetitions per set suit muscle growth; heavier loads with about 3-6 repetitions emphasise maximal strength; lighter loads with 15 or more repetitions emphasise muscular endurance. Rest of about 1-2 minutes between sets is typical for muscle growth, and longer (2-3 minutes or more) for heavy strength work.

## Progression
Progressive overload means gradually increasing load, repetitions or sets over weeks. A common approach is to add repetitions within a target range and increase the weight once the top of the range is reached with good form.

## Safety
Beginners should learn technique with light loads, warm up before training, and stop an exercise that causes sharp pain. People with injuries, joint problems or medical conditions should get advice from a physiotherapist or doctor before starting a resistance programme.
