## Benefits
Diets high in fiber are associated with lower risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes and colorectal cancer, better bowel regularity and greater fullness, which helps with weight management.

## Sources
Good sources include pulses (beans, lentils, chana), whole grains and millets, vegetables, fruits eaten whole, nuts and seeds. Refined grains such as white rice and maida, and fruit juices, have much less fiber than their whole-food versions.

## Increasing fiber
Increase fiber gradually and drink enough water to limit bloating. Swapping half of refined grains for whole grains or millets and adding a pulse dish to a meal can add 5-10 g of fiber per day.
